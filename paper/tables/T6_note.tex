$\hat\sigma$: sd of $\log(\hat p/p)$ on held-out A\_dev at $t=1$M, Poisson noise subtracted. Method B: per-password dispersion within bins of $\hat p$ over the passwords stored in the leak (alt. = account-weighted); s.e.\ over 5 folds. Method A: held-out vs model mass per bin over all strings (alt. = raw RMS, not Poisson-corrected); a lower bound on B. Bias $\mu=\log(\text{true}/\hat p)$, mass-weighted (B) or mean log-ratio (A); positive: $\hat p$ too small.
