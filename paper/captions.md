# Figure captions (factual drafts)

Generated by `experiments/paper_captions.py`; every number is computed from `results/*.csv` (recipes: `experiments/paper_numlib.py`). Not paper text.

**Common to all figures.** est = attacker weights p^ (the deployed estimate), candidate list = top 2M of S~. emp = attacker weights from leak counts, candidate list = **all of S~** (13,125,513 passwords, no generated strings). t = 1,000,000 (histogram head size) unless stated. Loss = bucket success - baseline (no-bucket) success of the same attacker, in percentage points (pp). Error bars: sd over 5 repeats of 5,000 targets (Fig. 2: sd over 5 noise draws). Seed 2000, pool A_test. Colour-blind-safe palette; every series also has its own line style and marker, so the figures read in grey scale. Each figure exists at column width (3.3 in, `figN`) and at 7 in (`figN_wide`), as PDF and 300-dpi PNG.

## Fig. 1 (`fig1`)

Loss against guess budget q (log scale) for M0 (a) and M2x, passwords of length >= 8 not on the Xato blocklist (b). FSB with qbar = 100 and size-matched HPB with l = 12 (average bucket 2,565 and 3,204 entries), against the est and emp attackers. Dotted vertical line: q = qbar. Dotted horizontal line: 5 points (drawn in both panels; it is the reference for the est curves). At q = qbar = 100 on M0: FSB est 0.00, FSB emp 10.92, HPB emp 40.90 pp; on M2x: FSB est 2.10, FSB emp 14.79, HPB emp 30.70 pp. Sources: `e1c_e2.csv` (est), `e1e_headline.csv` (emp).

## Fig. 2 (`fig2`)

Loss at q = qbar = 100 (M0, FSB) against the noise sigma that multiplies p^ by exp(sigma z_w), for noise on every string and for noise on the non-head (tail) strings only, est and emp. Points: mean over 5 independent noise draws (salts d1..d5), bars: sd over the draws; sigma = 0 is the noise-free run (no draws). Vertical dotted lines and top-axis labels: the measured estimator error sigma_hat on held-out data, head 0.308 and tail 1.570 (per-password dispersion, method B, t = 1M). Dotted horizontal line: 5 points. **Note: noise on the tail only leaves the emp attacker at the clean-estimate loss of 10.92 pp** (tail-only noise: 10.88 / 10.84 / 10.94 at sigma = 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.6), whereas noise on every string raises it to 12.92 / 19.60 / 33.03. For the est attacker tail-only noise gives -0.04 / +0.28 / +0.89 and full noise +2.63 / +9.77 / +23.20. Sources: `e1f_tailnoise.csv` (est), `e1f_tailnoise_emp.csv` (emp), `e1c_e2.csv` and `e1e_headline.csv` (sigma = 0), `e1d_esterror_summary.csv`.

## Fig. 3 (`fig3`)

Which targets the emp attacker gains on. Loss contribution = share of targets in the group x (attacker success - baseline success) within the group; the contributions of the three groups add up to the loss (diamond, with sd over repeats; the number above each bar is the total, pp of all targets). Groups: head = password among the top t = 1M by leak count (the histogram head); stored, not in head = in S~ but not in the head; not in S~ = no candidate (contribution 0). FSB qbar = 100 and HPB l = 12, q = 1 and q = 100, for M0 (a) and M2x (b). On M0 at q = 100 the stored-not-in-head group contributes +11.44 of the FSB total +10.92 (head -0.51); under HPB the head group contributes +40.90 of +40.90. Source: `e1f_mech_groups.csv`.

## Fig. 4 (`fig4`)

emp loss at q = qbar = 100 (FSB qbar = 100) against the histogram head size t (log scale), M0 (solid) and M2x (dashed); grey dotted curve, right axis: average bucket size. emp loss falls from 24.5 (M0) / 28.9 (M2x) at t = 100k to 2.3 / 4.3 at t = 10M. The est reference is **not a flat zero everywhere**: for M0 it is 0.00 at every t that was measured (100k to 3M; `e1c_tsweep.csv`; not measured at 10M), while for M2x the only est measurement is at t = 1M and is 2.10 pp (open square). Sources: `e1e_tsweep.csv`, `e1e_tsweep_10M.csv` (emp), `e1c_tsweep.csv` (est, M0), `e1c_e2.csv` (est, M2x).

## Fig. 5, appendix (`fig5`)

emp loss at q = 100 against the average bucket size (log scale) along a qbar sweep of 1 to 3000, for the base 3-gram (add-delta), the Kneser-Ney 3-gram, and the calibrated tail estimate (cal_iso_w0.5, exploratory: cross-fitted isotonic calibration of the tail, 0.5-decade bins), M0 (a) and M2x (b). The base and Kneser-Ney curves coincide (the base is drawn with larger markers underneath). Source: `e1e_fix.csv`.
